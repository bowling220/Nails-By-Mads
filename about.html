Frequently Asked Questions

What are your hours?

Appointment times are flexible as I do not have rigid set studio hours. We will coordinate a date and time that fits your schedule after you submit a booking request!

Do I need to send design inspiration first?

Yes! If you want specific gems, 3D gel, or detailed hand-painted designs, please let me know when booking so I can order any necessary supplies ahead of your appointment.

Can you travel to my location?

Absolutely! I am willing to travel to your location for appointments. Just mention your location in the notes when filling out your appointment request.

What is the food allergy policy?

Due to a personal wheat/gluten allergy, eating during appointments is not recommended for safety. If you have a specific medical need to bring food, please message me ahead of time!
